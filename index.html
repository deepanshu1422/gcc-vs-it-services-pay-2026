The Pay Map Nobody Hands You at Campus Placements: GCC vs IT Services

Same engineering degree, two very different offer letters. Here's the real data on who's actually hiring freshers in India right now, and what they pay for it.

The headline number

In FY26, India's Global Capability Centres (GCCs, the in-house India teams of multinational companies like Google, Walmart, Goldman Sachs) added an estimated 200,000 net tech jobs. India's own IT services giants (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech combined) added roughly 110,000, per TeamLease Digital CEO Neeti Sharma (quoted in The Times of India, June 2026).

Source: TeamLease Digital via Times of India (June 2026); Nasscom-Zinnov GCC Landscape Report 2026 (2,117 GCCs, 2.36 million people employed, $98.4B combined revenue, up 32% since FY2021).

IT services companies are hiring fewer freshers while adding record revenue (TCS added a net 17 employees in 9 months of FY26 while posting 10% YoY revenue growth). GCCs are doing the opposite: scaling headcount aggressively because AI-era work increasingly needs people who can own outcomes, not just execute tickets from a shared vendor pool.

5 companies with real GCC hiring momentum in India right now

  • Walmart Global Tech India — one of the largest GCC employers by headcount, active AI/data engineering hiring pipeline in Bengaluru.
  • Goldman Sachs Services India — large-scale GCC with consistent entry-level and lateral tech hiring across Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
  • Target in India (GCC) — growing engineering and data science teams, publicly discussed fresher programs.
  • JPMorgan Chase (India GCC) — one of the largest technology GCCs globally, continuous hiring across software and AI roles.
  • Google, Microsoft, Amazon India GCC/R&D units — smaller fresher intake than the above but the highest comp ceilings, selective but worth tracking via their careers pages directly.

This list reflects companies widely reported as active India GCC employers per 2026 industry coverage (Nasscom-Zinnov, Economic Times, TeamLease), not an exhaustive or ranked list. Always verify live openings directly on each company's careers page.

What their assessments actually test for

GCC hiring rounds for AI/GenAI-tagged roles lean harder on applied problem-solving with AI tools (using an LLM correctly, debugging its output, system-design judgment) rather than pure DSA-style coding rounds that dominate traditional IT services hiring. The skill that gets you into the ₹7L+ track isn't "I can use ChatGPT." It's "I can tell when the AI's answer is subtly wrong and fix it."
